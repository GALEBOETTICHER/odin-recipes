Pork Sinigang (Sinigang na Baboy)

Pork Sinigang (Sinigang na Baboy) is the ultimate Filipino comfort food, famous for its sour, savory broth packed with tender pork and crisp vegetables. The sourness traditionally comes from tamarind, which perfectly cuts through the richness of the pork fat.
